1. Who we are

Xtreme-Home Partner is the mobile app of Xtreme Facilities Management ("Xtreme", "we", "us"), Mazyad Offices T-1, Office 1102-4, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

This policy covers the customer app, the staff features in the same app, and our booking website and office systems that connect to it. We handle personal data in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service: create your account, take your booking, send the right team to the right address, and keep you updated. This is needed to perform our contract with you.
  • To take payment and issue refunds and VAT invoices. This is needed for the contract and to meet UAE tax law.
  • To keep accounts secure: verify sign-ins, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep audit records of changes made by our office.
  • To support you: answer questions and resolve complaints.
  • To improve our service: use ratings and booking history to train staff and plan teams.
  • To send notifications about your bookings. Offers are sent only if you agree, and you can switch notifications off in your phone's settings.

We do not use automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Location

Customers

If you allow it, the app reads your phone's location only while you are using it, to fill in your service address. You can type your address instead. The app does not track customers in the background.

Our staff

Staff members who sign in to the staff features share their location while they are on duty and travelling to a job, including when the app is in the background. This lets customers see the team arriving and lets our office dispatch the nearest team. Sharing stops when the staff member goes off duty. Location history is deleted automatically after 30 days.

5. Payments

Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are processed by Stripe. Pay-in-4 payments are processed by Tamara. You enter your payment details directly into their secure forms. Both are certified to the PCI DSS card security standard.

We receive only the payment result, the amount, and limited card details such as the brand and last four digits. We never receive or store your full card number or security code. Tamara may run its own checks before approving a pay-in-4 plan, under its own privacy policy.

6. Who we share it with

We never sell personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Our assigned team sees your name, mobile number, address and booking notes so they can reach you and do the job.
  • Service providers who work for us under contract and only on our instructions:
    • Stripe and Tamara (payments)
    • Twilio (SMS sign-in codes)
    • Google, Apple and Facebook (only if you choose to sign in with them)
    • Google Maps (maps in the app)
    • Expo, Apple and Google (push notifications)
    • Our cloud hosting provider (servers and database)
  • Authorities, when UAE law requires it, or to protect the safety of our customers and staff.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or merged. They must keep to this policy.

7. Where it is stored

Our servers and database are hosted in data centres in the European Union. Some of the providers listed above process data in other countries, including the United States. When data leaves the UAE, we rely on the safeguards allowed by UAE law, such as contracts with the provider that require an adequate level of protection.

8. How we protect it

  • All traffic between the app, our servers and our providers is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS).
  • Sign-in codes are stored only as one-way hashes, expire after 5 minutes, and lock after repeated wrong attempts.
  • Sign-in sessions use short-lived tokens that are revoked when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Office access is limited to authorised staff, and every change they make is recorded in an audit log.
  • Payment data is handled by PCI DSS certified providers, never by our servers.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the UAE Data Office as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under UAE data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete (you can edit most of it yourself in the app's Account tab)
  • delete your data (see section 11)
  • restrict or stop certain uses, and withdraw consent you gave earlier
  • send your data to you or another provider in a common format

Email us at the address below. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the UAE Data Office.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself at any time:

  1. Open Xtreme-Home Partner and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your name, mobile number, email, profile photo, saved addresses, sign-in links (Google, Apple, Facebook) and notification tokens are erased straight away, and you are signed out on all devices. Open bookings are cancelled, and paid ones are refunded.

We keep past booking and payment records without your name or contact details, only as long as UAE tax law requires.

If you can't open the app, or you signed in with Facebook and want your data removed, email sales@xtreme-fm.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the mobile number or email linked to your account. We will confirm within 30 days.

12. Children

The app is for adults aged 18 or over who book services for their home or business. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
